About Us

Learn more about SkillHive and our mission to connect skilled professionals

Our Mission

At SkillHive, our mission is to connect local professionals with people who need skilled services. We strive to make hiring trusted experts easy, fast, and reliable.

Our Vision

We envision a community where everyone can access professional skills locally, supporting small businesses and empowering skilled individuals to thrive.

Our Story

Founded in Malawi, SkillHive began as a platform to bridge the gap between skilled professionals and those seeking their services. Over time, we have grown to include a variety of trades and services including plumbing, electrical, welding, landscaping, and more.
